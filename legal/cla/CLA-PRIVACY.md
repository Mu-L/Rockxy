# Contributor Agreement Records Privacy Notice

Effective 2026-08-14

Rockxy records only the information reasonably needed to document contributor
agreement acceptance and contribution provenance. For GitHub-based acceptance,
that can include a GitHub login and numeric user ID, pull-request and comment
IDs, acceptance timestamp, agreement name and version, and the immutable
document reference used for acceptance.

The data controller for these records is **Nguyen Huu Loc**, also known
publicly as **Stephen**, the Project Owner identified by GitHub account
`LocNguyenHuu`. The controller can be contacted at `rockxyapp@gmail.com`.

Signature records are stored in the public Rockxy repository so recipients and
future maintainers can verify contribution provenance. GitHub independently
processes account, pull-request, and comment data under its own terms and
privacy notice.

Corporate agreements may additionally contain an entity name, registration
details, business address, representative name and title, signature, date, and
authorized-contributor list. Those executed agreements are retained privately
and are not published unless legally required or the entity agrees.

Records are retained for the life of the project and for as long afterward as
reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend intellectual-property
and contractual rights. They may be transferred to a successor that acquires
or operates Rockxy and assumes the relevant agreement obligations.

Questions or requests concerning these records may be sent to
`rockxyapp@gmail.com`. Some records cannot be deleted while Contributions that
rely on them remain in project history, but inaccurate account metadata can be
corrected with appropriate verification.
