# Contributor Agreement Records Privacy Notice

Effective 2026-08-14

Rockxy records only the information reasonably needed to document contributor
agreement acceptance and contribution provenance. For GitHub-based acceptance,
that can include a GitHub login and numeric user ID, pull-request and comment
IDs, acceptance timestamp, agreement name and version, and the immutable
document reference used for acceptance.

The data controller for these records is **Nguyen Huu Loc**, also known
publicly as **Stephen**, the Project Owner identified by GitHub account
`LocNguyenHuu`. The controller can be contacted at `rockxyapp@gmail.com`.

Signature records are stored in the public Rockxy repository so recipients and
future maintainers can verify contribution provenance. GitHub independently
processes account, pull-request, and comment data under its own terms and
privacy notice.

Where the EU GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the legal bases are performance of the
contributor agreement and the controller's legitimate interests in verifying
authorship, administering licenses, preventing fraud, and establishing,
exercising, or defending legal claims. A legal obligation may also apply when
records must be preserved or disclosed by law. Rockxy does not use these
records for automated decision-making, profiling, or marketing.

Corporate agreements may additionally contain an entity name, registration
details, business address, representative name and title, signature, date, and
authorized-contributor list. Those executed agreements are retained privately
and are not published unless legally required or the entity agrees.

Records are retained for the life of the project and for as long afterward as
reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend intellectual-property
and contractual rights. They may be transferred to a successor that acquires
or operates Rockxy and assumes the relevant agreement obligations.

Recipient categories are GitHub as repository host, the Project Owner and a
permitted successor, professional advisers who need the records for legal or
accounting work, and public authorities when disclosure is legally required.
Because GitHub and a future successor or adviser may process data in other
countries, applicable transfers will use a lawful transfer mechanism, such as
an adequacy decision or appropriate contractual safeguards. GitHub's own
processing and transfer safeguards are governed by its published privacy and
data-protection terms.

Questions or requests concerning these records may be sent to
`rockxyapp@gmail.com` with the subject `Contributor privacy request`. The
Project Owner may request enough information to verify the requester and will
acknowledge and respond without undue delay and, where the GDPR applies,
normally within one month. Depending on applicable law, a contributor may have
rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, and data
portability. Some records cannot be deleted while Contributions that rely on
them remain in project history or while retention is needed to establish or
defend legal rights, but inaccurate account metadata can be corrected with
appropriate verification. A contributor may complain to the data-protection
authority where they live or work, or where an alleged infringement occurred;
UK contributors may contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
