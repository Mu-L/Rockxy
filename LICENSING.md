# Rockxy Licensing

Rockxy has a public source edition and an official signed binary distribution.
They are related products, but they are not the same licensing artifact.

| Artifact | Applicable terms |
| --- | --- |
| Source in this public repository | GNU AGPL version 3 or later, except identified third-party material |
| A build made solely from this public repository | GNU AGPL version 3 or later |
| Official signed Rockxy DMG and app that includes or presents Binary EULA v1.0 | [Rockxy Binary EULA](legal/BINARY-EULA-v1.0.md) |
| Community mode in the official app | Free-of-charge runtime mode under the Binary EULA |
| Pro capabilities in the official app | Binary EULA plus the applicable purchase and entitlement terms |
| Third-party components | Their respective licenses and notices |
| Rockxy name and logos | No trademark license is granted by the AGPL or Binary EULA |

(`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). You may build, study, modify, and redistribute this
source under those terms.

## Official binary distribution

Official downloads published by Rockxy are signed and notarized binaries built
from a separate downstream distribution. They run in Community mode without a
purchase and can unlock paid Pro capabilities with an eligible entitlement.
Beginning with distributions that include or present Binary EULA v1.0 on or
after its effective date, the official DMG and app are licensed under the
[Rockxy Binary EULA](legal/BINARY-EULA-v1.0.md), except that third-party
components remain governed by their own licenses. This policy does not
retroactively change the license of an earlier distribution.

The public repository is not represented as the complete or exact source tree
used to build the official DMG. A public release tag identifies the public
Community source edition for that product version; it is not a representation
that the tag is the official DMG’s complete Corresponding Source.

The Binary EULA does not reduce rights granted in a separate copy of the public
AGPL source edition. Conversely, obtaining the public source edition does not
grant rights to private downstream components or paid service entitlements.

accepted Contribution to remain available in the public source edition while
